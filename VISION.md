# VISION: SM64 MMORPG Project

## Ultimate Goal
The ultimate goal of this project is to transform the Super Mario 64 PC port (sm64coopdx) into a massive, persistent, multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) that serves as a "Museum of Mechanics". This game will integrate the absolute best gameplay mechanics, traversal systems, combat concepts, and social features from the entire history of 3D platformers and action-adventure games into a single, cohesive, chaotic, and beautiful world.

## Core Pillars
1. **Unparalleled Movement:** Players should have access to Mario's classic moveset alongside FLUDD mechanics, Gliding, Hookshots, Vehicles, and Mounts. Traversal should be a joy.
2. **Deep RPG Systems:** A robust class system (Warrior/Mage/Rogue), branching talent trees, stat distribution (STR/INT/AGI), and a fully realized Universal Inventory with persistent storage (`mod_storage`).
3. **Thriving Player Economy:** Player-driven trading, a global asynchronous Auction House, NPC Faction shops, and physical crafting stations where raw materials gathered from the world (Fishing/Mining) are turned into valuable goods.
4. **Endless Content Depth:** Procedural scaling dungeons (Tower of Trials), synchronized Raid Bosses, Daily Quests, and intricate puzzles.
5. **Robust Social Infrastructure:** Guilds, Parties, persistent Mailboxes, Player Housing (instanced), and an integrated achievement system with cosmetic titles.
6. **Flawless User Experience:** A centralized, unified `UIToolkit` that renders all menus with descriptive labels, wrapping text, and detailed hover-tooltips for every item, stat, and ability. An in-game Encyclopedia must always be available to explain mechanics.
7. **Bulletproof Architecture:** Headless server support, authoritative server-side anti-cheat (rubber-banding/kicking), interest management for high player counts, and clean Lua APIs designed for maximum extensibility.

## The Future
We are building a world that supports thousands of concurrent players. Every feature implemented must be scalable, documented in extreme detail, and thoroughly tested for multiplayer synchronization. We will not stop until every planned feature is 100% complete and fully represented in the UI.
