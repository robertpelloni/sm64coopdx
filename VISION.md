# sm64coopdx MMORPG Vision

## The Ultimate Goal
To create the definitive Multiplayer 3D Platforming experience, transforming Super Mario 64 into a living, breathing MMORPG world that honors the history of the genre.

## Core Pillars

1.  **The "Museum of Mechanics"**:
    *   We don't just add features; we curate the best mechanics from 3D platformer history (Mario, Banjo, Spyro, Ratchet, Jak, Psychonauts) and integrate them seamlessly into Mario's moveset.
    *   Every mechanic must feel native, polished, and useful.

2.  **Persistent World**:
    *   Players earn progress that matters.
    *   Inventory, Quests, Guilds, and Economy are the backbone of long-term engagement.
    *   The world is connected; no more level select screens. Walk from Bob-omb Battlefield to Whomp's Fortress.

3.  **Massive Scale**:
    *   Technical architecture designed for hundreds/thousands of concurrent players.
    *   Headless servers, Instancing, and Interest Management are critical technical hurdles to solve.

4.  **Social Gameplay**:
    *   Cooperation is key. Raid Bosses and Dungeons require tank/healer/dps coordination (Warrior/Mage/Rogue).
    *   Trading and Economy drive player interaction.

## Design Philosophy
*   **"If it's not fun, it's not in."**
*   **"Documentation is part of the feature."** - If a player doesn't know how to use it, it doesn't exist.
*   **"Robustness."** - Input safety, network sync, and error handling are paramount.

## Future Horizons
*   **Procedural Dungeons**: Infinite replayability.
*   **Player Housing**: Instanced personal spaces.
*   **Cross-Game Content**: Importing levels from other N64 games (Ocarina of Time, Banjo-Kazooie) as expansion zones.
